Our Process

From brief to deployment

  1. 01
    Requirements Mapping

    We map your recruiting workflow, define scoring criteria, and align on integration points before writing a line of code.

  2. 02
    Algorithm Design

    We design the AI logic — scoring models, ranking heuristics, and matching engines — tailored to your specific talent criteria.

  3. 03
    Build & Integrate

    We build the tool and connect it to your existing systems — ATS, HRIS, or custom platforms — via clean API interfaces.

  4. 04
    Test & Validate

    We run the tool against real candidate data, tune scoring accuracy, and validate outcomes with your recruiting team.

  5. 05
    Deploy & Train

    We deploy to your environment, hand over full ownership of the codebase, and train your team to use and maintain it.
